How to use this log

Log a long run

Tap any week card to open it. Pick the day you ran, then enter your distance and time. Tap the circle on the right to mark the week done — it turns green and fills your progress bar.

Entering time & pace

Type time as hh:mm:ss (e.g. 1:45:30) or just mm:ss (e.g. 52:10). Your pace is worked out automatically from distance ÷ time and charted up top — lower is faster.

Add an extra run

Ran twice in a week, or did a midweek jog? Open Extra runs ▸ + Add, set the date and distance (time and a note are optional). Extra runs count toward your total distance and the charts, but not the “/26 long runs done” count.

Races & events

Use Races & Events ▸ + Add for a race. A race replaces that week's long run — don't do both. After you run it, open the event card and log the distance and time so it counts.

Weight & accent colour

Enter your weight in any week to build the trend line at the top. Tap the colour swatch in the header to recolour the whole app.

Where your data lives

Everything saves automatically on this device, in your browser. There's no account and no sync between devices — clearing your browser's site data will erase your log, so don't clear it if you want to keep your history.
