How to use this log

Log every run

The big + Log a run button on the dashboard is the heart of the app. Date, distance, time, type, notes — every run counts toward your stats and charts, no matter what.

Runs fill your goal automatically

If a run lands inside the active goal's current training week, it ticks off that week's next open workout automatically — even if the distance differs from the plan. You'll see planned vs. actual, never a nag. Extra runs beyond the week's slots count as bonus volume.

Entering time & pace

Type time as h:mm:ss (e.g. 1:45:30) or just mm:ss (e.g. 52:10). Pace is always worked out from distance ÷ time — lower is faster.

Goals & events

Create a goal in the Goals tab — pick a race distance and date and a progressive plan is generated, with cutback weeks and a taper. Add a race or event to any goal: by default it replaces that week's long run, just like your July 5 10K.

Weight & accent colour

Log your weight in the Weight tab to build the trend line. Change the accent colour above to recolour the whole app.

Where your data lives

Everything saves automatically on this device, in your browser. No account, no sync — clearing the browser's site data erases your log. Your pre-update data was backed up on this device the first time this version loaded.
